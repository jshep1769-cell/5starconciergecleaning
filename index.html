Cleaning Options to Discuss

These descriptions explain cleaning requests you may want to discuss with a provider. They are not a promise of available services or included tasks. Our provider connections are still being arranged.

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

When planning a move, ask about cleaning an empty home, including cabinet and drawer interiors, the refrigerator and the oven. Confirm whether these tasks are included, priced separately or excluded, and what access the provider needs.

Deep Cleaning

Ask about detailed attention to baseboards, window sills, cabinet exteriors, kitchen and bathroom surfaces, vacuuming and mopping. The provider should assess the home and explain the scope, suitable methods and any extra charges before work begins.

Maintenance Cleaning

For ongoing upkeep, discuss weekly, every-other-week or monthly visits. Ask which tasks are included and whether an initial deep clean is required. Frequency, pricing and availability depend on the provider.

Cleaning topics to discuss:

  • Complete Kitchen Cleaning

  • Window cleaning: confirm scope with a provider

  • Floor cleaning

  • Top to bottom bathrooms

  • Dusting, tidying, and vacuuming other rooms

  • Surface cleaning and disinfecting options

  • Ask about tasks specific to your home

Planning a cleaning? List your rooms, priorities and preferred timing before speaking with a provider.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide the cleaning yourselves?

No. This is an independently operated referral website. We do not employ or dispatch cleaners, and we are not the former business that used this domain.

Can I book a cleaning through this website?

Not currently. Provider connections are being arranged, and we cannot book or quote cleaning services. When referrals become available, the independent provider will handle quotes, appointments and service agreements.

Do I need to be home during cleaning?

Confirm access arrangements directly with the provider you select. Do not send keys, door codes or alarm details to this referral website.

How should I prepare for a cleaning visit?

Ask the provider whether personal items and clutter need to be put away, whether tidying costs extra, and how pets should be managed during the visit. Discuss fragile items and surfaces needing special care.

Are providers background-checked and insured?

We have not verified provider credentials and do not make that guarantee. Ask the provider about screening, current insurance and any credentials relevant to the work before booking.

Who supplies products and equipment?

Ask whether the provider brings supplies and equipment or expects you to provide them. Discuss product preferences and any household sensitivities directly with the provider; we do not guarantee that products are eco-friendly or suitable for every household.

What about carpet cleaning, window washing or special requests?

Ask whether the provider accepts your specific task and what it includes. Carpet cleaning and window washing may require separate arrangements. These services are not confirmed offerings through this website.

What are the cancellation and payment terms?

The independent provider sets its own cancellation, rescheduling, payment and refund terms. Request those details before agreeing to service. The former business’s policies do not apply to referrals from this website.

Can I arrange recurring cleaning?

Ask a provider about available schedules, included tasks and prices. We do not promise recurring availability, discounted rates or priority appointments.
